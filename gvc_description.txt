GenerativeVideoCompiler is an experimental video art script, or environment, that transforms found footage into a layered, temporal collage. It operates by first scouring a curated directory of video frames. Through a randomized yet controlled process, the app selects a sequence of frames, ranging from 10 to 75, to form the base of a visual sequence. These sequences are then algorithmically layered, introducing a crafted opacity and employing blending modes. This method is akin to a digital form of decoupage, piecing together disparate moments into a cohesive whole.

The blended images, seen as "noise," undergo a diffusion process. This is not merely a technical step but an artistic one, where the "noise" is interpreted and reimagined into imagery - recognizable elements like human figures, architectural elements, landscapes, cityscapes, objects, geometric shapes, or abstract patterns a process reminiscent of finding shapes in clouds. This feature allows for art to emerge from the chaos.

GenerativeVideoCompiler is a digital crucible for video artistry and reinterpretation of visual data. It challenges the boundaries between the found and the created, between the past and the present, and ultimately between noise and signal.
